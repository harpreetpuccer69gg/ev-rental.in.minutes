2. What We Have Built

A fully responsive EV rental discovery web app for Flipkart Minutes delivery riders with the following features:

  • Hero Carousel — 3 posters: Delivery Rider intro, YuvwaaSpeed special offer (₹59/day), Bounce EV poster
  • City Selector — 13 cities: Bangalore, Mumbai, Delhi NCR, Chennai, Kolkata, Hyderabad, Pune, Ahmedabad, Jaipur, Lucknow, Guwahati, Patna, Coimbatore
  • Budget Filter — 4 ranges: Below ₹1000 / ₹1000–1500 / ₹1500–2000 / ₹2000+
  • EV Results — Search bar, filter by type (Hi-Speed / Low Speed / E-Cycle), sort by price
  • EV Detail Page — Slide-in page showing range, charging type, security deposit, refundable deposit, weekly rent
  • Booking Form — Rider enters name and phone number, vendor contacts within 24 hours
  • Multilingual — English, Hindi, Bengali, Kannada
  • Intro Popup — Language and city selection on first visit
  • Auto Lead Capture — Every booking auto-logs to Google Sheets
  • Mobile Responsive — Works perfectly on any Android or iPhone browser
  • 40+ Vendor Entries across 13 cities including Bounce, YuvwaaSpeed, Yulu, Freedo, Bijliride, Bykemania, Volt Up, EMO, Linkmiles, Nexzu, Motovolt, TezzFleet, Zeway and more

5. Conclusion

The EV Assist - Flipkart Minutes platform is a complete, production-deployed web solution that connects Flipkart delivery riders with EV rental vendors across 13 Indian cities.

Riders can visit the landing page, select their city and budget, browse available EVs with full pricing transparency, and submit a booking request in under 60 seconds — all from their mobile browser without downloading any app.

Every lead is automatically captured in Google Sheets, giving the operations team real-time visibility into rider demand, city-wise interest, and vendor performance.

The platform is live, scalable, and ready to onboard more vendors and cities as Flipkart Minutes expands.
